What respondents told us

Themes pulled from the open-text responses

What worked
  • Pair (AI) assistant — named by several respondents as the single biggest help for step-by-step guidance during exercises.
  • Left navigation column and overall UI called out as intuitive.
  • DTO team support during troubleshooting — one shoutout to "Wan Ling" by name.
  • The café dataset exercise and hands-on group format were repeatedly praised as engaging.
  • Respondents who finished the DataCamp pre-work said it made the live session easier to follow.
Opportunities to improve
  • DataCamp platform stability — multiple reports of lagging, refreshing, and repeating tasks; one respondent found the instructions misleading.
  • More hands-on time requested, especially for troubleshooting and the SQL → dashboard visualization step.
  • Pair assistant reliability — one respondent hit code errors and suggested further training/refinement of the AI.
  • One request to enable the "Genie" feature.
  • Laptop performance issues made it hard for at least one participant to keep pace.
"Love the café idea. The pair chat was also very useful — though there may be a data integrity issue if we use actual data and don't understand what the data cleaning is doing."
